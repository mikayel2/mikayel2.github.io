## Overview

Led a team of engineers in developing an autonomous UV sterilization robot designed to disinfect environments such as hospitals, schools and offices. **Won 1st place award for "UVON Desk" project at Fab Lab Armenia.**

## Key Achievements

- **Navigation System**: Integrated LiDAR-based SLAM algorithm for autonomous navigation on an onboard NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX computer

- **Validation Research**: Tested and validated the UV system's effectiveness through controlled laboratory experiments on E. coli bacteria, demonstrating reliable disinfection performance

- **Scientific Research**: Conducted research on germicidal properties of UVC light emitted from HID Mercury Vapor lamps with bacteriological analysis

## Award Recognition

**1st Place Award** - Fab Lab Armenia for "UVON Desk" project
*Inspection of the Germicidal Properties of UVC Light Emitted from HID Mercury Vapor Lamps on the Example of Escherichia Coli Inactivation*

## Technical Stack

- **Hardware**: NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX, LiDAR sensors, UVC lamps
- **Algorithms**: SLAM, autonomous navigation
- **Research**: Bacteriological analysis, germicidal properties study
- **Applications**: Hospital, school, and office disinfection

## Project Timeline

**Duration**: January 2021 - June 2022

This startup project combined robotics, computer vision, and medical research to create a practical solution for autonomous disinfection in critical environments.
