## Overview

Six month global program by MIT's Center for Bits and Atoms focused on digital fabrication, rapid prototyping, and hands-on engineering. Learned to design, prototype, and fabricate smart devices integrating mechanical, electronic, and software components by completing weekly projects.

### Digital Fabrication
- **PCB Design**: Circuit board design and fabrication
- **3D Modeling**: CAD design and 3D printing
- **Laser Cutting**: Precision cutting and engraving
- **Embedded Systems**: Microcontroller programming and integration

### Programming & Tools
- **Languages**: C/C++, Python, embedded C
- **Design Software**: Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Eagle, KiCad
- **Hardware**: Arduino, various microcontrollers
- **Fabrication**: Digital fabrication workflows

## Program Structure

The program consisted of weekly hands-on projects that progressively built skills in:
- Electronics design and fabrication
- Mechanical design and manufacturing
- Software development for embedded systems
- System integration and testing

## Learning Outcomes

- Gained comprehensive experience in the full product development cycle
- Developed skills in rapid prototyping and iterative design
- Learned to integrate mechanical, electronic, and software components
- Built foundation for future innovation in digital fabrication

## Project Timeline

**Duration**: January 2020 - June 2020

This intensive program provided the foundation for future projects in robotics, embedded systems, and digital fabrication, directly contributing to later successes in autonomous systems development.
