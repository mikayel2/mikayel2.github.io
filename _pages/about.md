#### About me
Hey, I am a 3rd year Ph.D. student in the Mechanical Engineering department at the <a href='https://illinois.edu/'> University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign</a>, working with Professor <a href='https://naira-hovakimyan.mechse.illinois.edu/'> Naira Hovakimyan</a>. 

#### Research Statement
I work at the intersection of multi-agent optimization, control theory, robotics, and machine learning for safety-critical autonomy. My focus is on scalable consensus algorithms with formal guarantees, ensuring heterogeneous UAVs (Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, drones), remain robust under communication loss, uncertainty, and disturbance. By uniting rigorous theory with real-world experiments, my research advances autonomy for applications like package delivery, wildfire suppression, and aerial surveillance.

I am looking for internship or co-op positions starting in Jan 2026 or May 2026.

#### Experience

**Apple** | *Controls and Simulation Intern* | May 2024 – August 2024  
*Platform Architecture, Cupertino, CA*

Developed scalable, high-fidelity models for predicting temperature in System-on-Chip (SoC) architectures, which integrate multiple computing components such as CPU, GPU, and memory onto a single chip. Combined both physics-based and data-driven machine learning methods, with validation in closed-loop settings to ensure accuracy and robustness under realistic operating conditions.

- Designed and benchmarked gray-box thermal models, linear/nonlinear ARX models, and AI methods such as deep neural networks (DNNs) and physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)
- Developed an end-to-end pipeline for collecting training datasets, preprocessing, model training, validation, and deployment of closed-loop simulations (Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Achieved reliable SoC temperature prediction with lumped-element thermal models, reaching ~80% out-of-sample (OOS) accuracy and reducing error by 15–20% compared to baselines

**Aerial Robotics Laboratory (NPUA)** | *Controls and Simulation Engineer* | January 2021 – July 2022  
*Yerevan, Armenia*

- Developed and integrated flight control software for autonomous drones using C++, MATLAB/Simulink to implement and evaluate control system algorithms (PID, Geometric, L1 Adaptive)
- Utilized software-in-the-loop (SIL) simulation to debug, fine-tune, and validate controller performance prior to real-world flight tests
- Conducted real-world flight experiments to assess robustness and adaptability under challenging conditions, including added payloads and wind disturbances

#### Projects

**[Autonomous Vehicle Summoning (Course Project)](https://github.com/krishauser/GEMstack/tree/summoning_integration)**

Developed a full-stack autonomous vehicle summoning system featuring path generation, obstacle detection, self-parking and a mobile user interface, fully integrated into the ROS-based GEMstack software stack and conduct in-vehicle tests on GEMe2.

- Implemented motion planning for collision-free path generation using RRT* and Reeds–Shepp curves for autonomous parking and summoning maneuvers
- Integrated real-time obstacle detection using YOLO-based perception pipeline for enhanced safety and situational awareness, utilizing onboard cameras and LiDAR
- Developed a mobile application enabling users to specify pick-up and drop-off locations, interfacing with the autonomous vehicle system (GEMstack)
