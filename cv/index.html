Summary I work at the intersection of multi-agent optimization, control theory, robotics, and machine learning for safety-critical autonomy. My focus is on scalable consensus algorithms with formal guarantees, ensuring heterogeneous UAVs (Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, drones), remain robust under communication loss, uncertainty, and disturbance. By uniting rigorous theory with real-world experiments, my research advances autonomy for applications like package delivery, wildfire suppression, and aerial surveillance.
